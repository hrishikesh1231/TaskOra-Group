# 🚀 TaskOra – Hyperlocal Task & Service Marketplace

# 🌟 Overview

TaskOra is a full-stack MERN application that connects people who need help with people willing to provide services. The platform enables users to post tasks, discover opportunities, apply for work, communicate securely, and build trust through ratings and reviews.

Unlike traditional job portals, TaskOra is designed for **any type of local need**, including daily wage work, student assistance, event support, household services, tutoring, project help, and professional services.

The platform focuses on solving the gap between people who need help and people willing to provide it within their local communities.

---

# 💡 Problem Statement

Finding trustworthy local help for short-term tasks is often difficult.

People face challenges such as:

* Lack of a dedicated platform for local task-based work
* Difficulty finding nearby workers quickly
* Trust and safety concerns
* Fake or harmful task postings
* Limited opportunities for students and freelancers
* Poor communication between task posters and applicants

---

# ✅ Our Solution

TaskOra provides a smart hyperlocal marketplace where users can:

* Post tasks and services
* Discover opportunities nearby
* Apply for gigs and jobs
* Communicate securely
* Build reputation through ratings and reviews
* Use AI-powered content moderation
* Purchase tokens for platform activities

The platform combines marketplace functionality, AI validation, authentication, payments, and reputation systems into one ecosystem.
